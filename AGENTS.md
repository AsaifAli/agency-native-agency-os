## Organizational model
The founder is at the top, followed by the Chief of Staff, department supervisors, specialist agents/sub-agents and human contributors. These are distinct workforce members with explicit reporting lines, status, autonomy and capabilities.

Revenue and Delivery are operating domains, not separate authority systems. The OS owns identity, assignments, permissions, tasks, approvals, budgets, sessions, artifacts and audit.

## Non-negotiable
- Founder authority is supreme and cannot be delegated to an agent.
- A role, title, prompt or skill never grants permissions.
- Every tool action is authorized by backend policy for actor, organization, workspace, data class and action.
- Sensitive communications, pricing/contracts, payments, production releases, destructive actions, permission changes and client-data deletion require the appropriate approval.
- Stop on opt-out, reply, bounce, policy violation, expired authorization or cancellation.
- Do not claim a capability is live because a role or skill document exists.
- Do not activate provider runtimes until the separate Claude setup milestone is configured and verified.

## Agent profile format
Use [agents/AGENT_TEMPLATE.md](agents/AGENT_TEMPLATE.md). A profile describes purpose, reporting line, scope, autonomy, skills, tools requested, data eligibility, approval boundaries, KPIs and escalation. Tool permissions are configured in the OS, never in Markdown.

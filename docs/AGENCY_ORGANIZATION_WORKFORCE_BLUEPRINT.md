# Agency Organization & Workforce Blueprint

## Governance principle
Claude executes bounded work. Agency OS owns identity, permissions, business state, budgets, artifacts, audit and approvals. The founder retains final authority. All traditional departments remain present; AI reduces coordination overhead rather than deleting functions.

## Office of the CEO
- Founder/CEO: strategy, final commercial authority, protected approvals, financial commitments, hiring decisions, risk appetite and production releases.
- Chief of Staff / Agency Supervisor: converts founder priorities into plans, routes work to department heads, monitors dependencies, prepares decision briefs and escalates exceptions. Cannot grant itself permissions or approve protected actions.
- Executive Assistant: calendar, briefing packs, reminders and meeting administration; no external commitments.

## Group A — Growth
### 1. Marketing & Brand
Positions: Head of Marketing, Brand Strategist, Content Strategist, Content Specialist, SEO Specialist, Social Media Manager, Growth Marketing Specialist, Marketing Analyst.
Responsibilities: positioning, messaging, campaigns, content, SEO, case studies, channel analytics and attribution.
Outputs: approved messaging, campaign plans, content assets and channel reports.
KPIs: qualified marketing leads, channel conversion, organic visibility, engagement, cost per qualified lead and campaign ROI.
Boundary: founder approves major positioning changes and significant spend.

### 2. Business Development & Sales
Positions: Head of BD, Lead Generation Specialist, Lead Research Analyst, SDR, BD Executive, Account Executive, CRM Manager, Proposal Specialist.
Responsibilities: prospect research, qualification, CRM hygiene, outreach preparation, pipeline, discovery scheduling and proposal coordination.
Outputs: provenance-backed leads, qualification summaries, relationship history, forecasts and proposal drafts.
KPIs: qualified opportunities, pipeline coverage, meeting-to-discovery conversion, proposal conversion, win rate and sales-cycle duration.
Boundary: sender approval, consent/provenance, suppression and rate limits required; pricing, discounts, scope commitments and contracts need founder approval.

### 3. Market Research & Intelligence
Positions: Research Director, Industry Research Analyst, Competitor Intelligence Analyst, Technology Market Analyst, Opportunity Analyst, Research Quality Reviewer.
Responsibilities: market and sector research, competitor intelligence, technology/provider analysis, pricing research and opportunity analysis.
Outputs: sourced briefs, competitor matrices, opportunity maps and recommendations with confidence/limitations.
KPIs: source quality, freshness, accuracy, turnaround and research-to-action conversion.

## Group B — Strategy & Commercial
### 4. Solutions Consulting & Strategy
Positions: Head of Solutions, Business Analyst, AI Solutions Architect, Automation Consultant, Data Solutions Consultant, Presales Consultant, Proposal Architect.
Responsibilities: discovery, process mapping, requirements, feasibility, architecture options, estimates and proposal/SOW drafts.
Outputs: discovery brief, requirements register, architecture, assumptions/exclusions, estimates and acceptance criteria.
KPIs: discovery-to-proposal time, estimate accuracy, proposal turnaround, requirements completeness and scope-change rate.
Boundary: designs/recommends; Engineering implements and QA independently verifies.

### 5. Finance & Accounts
Positions: Finance Controller, Accountant, Financial Analyst, Budget Analyst, Revenue Analyst, Accounts Receivable Specialist, Accounts Payable Specialist.
Responsibilities: management accounts, budgets, project profitability, cash visibility, receivables/payables and forecasts.
Outputs: financial pack, margin view, cash forecast, ageing report and budget variance.
KPIs: margin visibility, overdue receivables, cash forecast accuracy, budget variance and cost completeness.
Boundary: payments, refunds, write-offs, tax filings and binding financial commitments require founder approval and appropriate professional review.

### 6. Legal, Risk & Compliance
Positions: Legal & Compliance Lead, Contract Analyst, Privacy Analyst, Compliance Analyst, IP & Licensing Analyst, Risk Analyst.
Responsibilities: contract review coordination, NDA/clause summaries, licensing/IP checks, privacy assessments and risk register.
Outputs: issue lists, draft clause suggestions, licensing register, privacy/risk assessment and escalations.
KPIs: unresolved high risks, review turnaround, licensing coverage and overdue mitigations.
Boundary: AI assists research/drafting; it is not legal counsel and cannot sign or make legal commitments.

Responsibilities: model/framework evaluation, experiments, benchmark design, research prototypes and transition recommendations.
Outputs: reproducible experiment records, benchmark reports, model selection notes and reusable research assets.
KPIs: validated experiments, repeatability, benchmark coverage, research reuse and research-to-engineering adoption.

### 8. AI Engineering & Development

Responsibilities: product implementation, APIs, UI, data pipelines, model integration, maintainability, documentation and tests.
Outputs: reviewed code, tested services, deployment packages, technical docs and implementation evidence.
KPIs: defect rate, cycle time, test coverage, maintainability, performance and rework.

### 9. Automation & Integration Engineering
Positions: Head of Automation, Workflow Automation Engineer, Integration Engineer, MCP Engineer, Voice Automation Engineer, Process Automation Analyst, Automation QA Specialist.
Responsibilities: controlled integrations, API adapters, MCP servers, event consumers, webhooks, document flows, telephony adapters and process automation.
Outputs: versioned integration specs, idempotent adapters, workflow tests, recovery instructions and health reports.
KPIs: success rate, duplicate side effects, recovery time, hours saved, incidents and exception rate.

### 10. Infrastructure & IT Operations
Positions: IT Operations Lead, Cloud/DevOps Engineer, Platform Engineer, Database Administrator, Site Reliability Engineer, Security Operations Analyst, IT Support Specialist.
Responsibilities: environments, deployment, databases, backup/restore, observability, secrets operations, incident response and internal IT.

## Group D — Execution & Quality
### 11. Project Management Office (PMO)
Positions: Head of PMO, Project Manager, Project Coordinator, Resource Planner, Risk & Dependency Analyst, Project Reporting Analyst.
Responsibilities: project plans, work breakdown, dependencies, milestones, capacity, status, risk tracking and change control.

### 12. Delivery & Implementation

Responsibilities: onboarding execution, configuration, migration, deployment coordination, implementation evidence, enablement and handover.

### 13. Quality Assurance & Security

## Reporting and low-bureaucracy coordination
Founder/CEO is final authority. Group heads report to founder; department heads report to group heads. When workload is small, a group head may temporarily coordinate a department, but that department remains a distinct responsibility, skill and KPI owner. Chief of Staff maintains one cross-group priority queue and exception brief. PMO owns schedule/dependency visibility; functional heads own work quality/capacity. QA/Security is independent. Finance and Legal/Risk independently surface risk. Client Success owns relationship; PMO/Delivery own execution.

## Position vs agent vs session
A position is a durable organizational responsibility. An agent definition is a versioned instruction/runtime/permission profile. An agent session executes a task. Do not deploy one always-on agent per title. Start with department-head/shared role agents; add specialist agents when volume, quality or isolation justifies it. All departments and roles remain represented in the organization model.

## Standard position record
Stable key; group; department; title; purpose; reports-to; responsibilities; skill requirements and versions; allowed tools/data classes/scopes; autonomy ceiling; budgets/concurrency; reviewers/escalations; output schema; quality criteria; KPIs; lifecycle state; revision history.

## Autonomy levels
- L0 Observe: read scoped data and report.
- L1 Prepare: analyze, recommend and draft; no side effects.
- L2 Internal execution: explicitly allowlisted internal changes.
- L3 Controlled external execution: preauthorized routine work with approved sender, consent/provenance, suppression, contact windows, rate limits and stop-on-reply/opt-out/bounce.
- L4 Founder decision: pricing, discounts, contracts/SOW, payments/refunds, sensitive communications, production releases, destructive actions, security/permission changes, client-data deletion, major spend, strategy and exceptions.
Unknown actions default to manual review. Backend policy enforces ceilings; prompts cannot grant permission.

## Cross-department flow
Market Research → Marketing → BD/Sales → Solutions Consulting → Finance + Legal review → Founder commercial approval → PMO → Engineering/Automation → QA/Security → Delivery → Client Success → Finance receivables/project margin. Chief of Staff monitors and escalates exceptions only.

## OS module mapping
Organization & Workforce; Agent Registry; Revenue Engine; Solutions & Commercial; Delivery Engine; Finance; Approval Center; Execution Engine; Integration Hub; Knowledge & Artifacts; Evaluation & QA; Operations & Monitoring. Do not create sixteen separate applications.

**Implementation status:** target operating model; not evidence that every position, agent, workflow, API or UI is already implemented.

## Assignment rules
A skill is reusable instruction, not an agent, tool, permission, runtime or approval. The learning and Cowork skills are operating guidance only; they do not create persistent memory, provider sessions, connectors or tool grants. All catalog skills are disabled by default and have no tool grants. Workforce profiles may recommend skill IDs; workforce assignment and provider injection are not enabled until a founder-authorized assignment model and runtime adapter are implemented and tested.

# Finance and Operations Analysis

Use for internal budgeting, project-cost analysis, estimates, receivables visibility, and management reporting.

1. Identify the reporting period, workspace/project, currency, and decision being supported.
2. Use recorded estimates, approved fees, invoices, payments, expenses, and provider usage. Flag missing or provisional data.
3. Store and calculate money in integer minor units with explicit ISO currency codes.
4. Never add unlike currencies without a documented conversion rate, source, timestamp, and conversion record.
5. Reconcile totals to source records and show formulas, assumptions, exclusions, and incomplete cost allocations.
6. Distinguish budget, estimate, committed cost, actual cost, invoiced amount, receivable, and cash received.
7. Label margin and profitability as provisional when costs or revenue allocations are incomplete.
8. Identify variance drivers, overdue items, cost anomalies, and forecast uncertainty.
9. Do not initiate payments, refunds, write-offs, transfers, or financial commitments.
10. This workflow is management analysis, not statutory accounting, tax advice, audit, payroll, or legal advice. Escalate to the founder and qualified professionals.

Output: a reconciled summary, variance table, assumptions, risks, and recommended review actions.

# Client Discovery and Requirements

Use for initial discovery, solution consulting, onboarding, and change-request analysis.

1. Establish the client's business outcome, current process, users, stakeholders, and decision process.
2. Capture the problem before proposing a solution. Distinguish symptoms, root causes, and desired outcomes.
3. Record functional requirements, non-functional requirements, data sources, integrations, security/privacy needs, constraints, and dependencies.
4. Give every requirement a stable ID and a clear rationale, priority, owner, and measurable acceptance criterion.
5. Identify assumptions, exclusions, unknowns, risks, and questions that need client or founder confirmation.
6. Map requirements to candidate deliverables and tests without silently expanding scope.
7. Distinguish verbatim client statements from AI summaries and inferred requirements.
8. Confirm understanding through a concise playback and record unresolved decisions.
9. Do not promise timelines, pricing, feasibility, compliance, or contractual scope. Escalate commitments for founder approval.

Output: discovery brief, requirement register, acceptance criteria, risks, assumptions, and decision log.

# Client Communication Drafting

Use for emails, meeting follow-ups, status updates, onboarding messages, and support responses.

1. Identify recipient, relationship, purpose, desired next step, and communication channel.
2. Use only approved facts from the correct lead/client workspace. Never expose another client's information.
3. Check contact preference, consent/provenance, suppression, reply, opt-out, bounce, complaint, and stop status before recommending outreach.
4. Use a concise, respectful, human tone. State what is known, what is uncertain, and what action is requested.
5. Do not invent promises, dates, pricing, performance claims, legal positions, or commitments.
6. For support or delivery updates, distinguish verified progress from plans, estimates, blockers, and risks.
7. Draft the message and identify any missing approval or evidence.
8. Do not send or schedule communications. Sensitive, commercial, unusual, or externally binding messages require founder approval.
9. Stop follow-ups when a reply, opt-out, bounce, complaint, suppression, or founder instruction is recorded.

Output: draft, recipient/context check, factual basis, and approval requirement.

# Operational Learning Capture

## Purpose
Use after a completed or failed execution, client milestone, sales experiment, incident, evaluation run or recurring review. The goal is to improve future decisions without turning guesses or client-specific information into universal policy.

## Capture procedure
1. **Anchor the event.** Reference the OS execution, task, project, opportunity, evaluation or incident ID. Record time, workspace, responsible owner and outcome.
2. **Separate evidence from interpretation.** Capture observed facts and links/artifact references separately from hypotheses, opinions and recommendations.
3. **State the learning.** Describe what was expected, what happened, why the difference matters, and what would falsify the conclusion.
4. **Bound applicability.** Tag department, workflow, service, model/runtime, data class, client/workspace scope and validity period. Default client-derived learning to that workspace only.
5. **Record confidence.** Use evidence strength, sample size, recency and counterexamples. Do not infer causation from correlation or one anecdote.
6. **Recommend a change.** Suggest a skill, checklist, test, workflow or evaluation update. Do not silently edit approved operating policy.
7. **Review and promote.** A designated human reviews sensitive or cross-client lessons. Founder approval is required to promote a lesson into organization-wide policy or reusable cross-workspace knowledge.
8. **Re-evaluate.** Attach later outcomes and mark lessons stale, contradicted or superseded when evidence changes.

## Data and privacy rules
Do not copy secrets, raw personal data, confidential client artifacts or regulated information into shared knowledge. Store references to access-controlled artifacts rather than duplicating content. Apply retention and deletion requirements to both the learning record and linked artifacts.

## Output contract
Return: source event; evidence; interpretation; confidence; scope; proposed operational change; reviewer; status (candidate, reviewed, promoted, superseded); expiry/review date. A captured lesson is not automatically an instruction or permission.

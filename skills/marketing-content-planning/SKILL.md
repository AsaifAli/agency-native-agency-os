# Marketing Content Planning

Use for internal marketing strategy, campaign briefs, thought leadership, website content, and social content planning.

1. Define the audience, problem, service fit, buying stage, and intended action.
2. Ground positioning in approved agency capabilities, delivered evidence, and differentiators.
3. Build a message hierarchy: audience pain, outcome, approach, proof, limitations, and call to action.
4. Select channels and formats based on audience behavior and available capacity, not vanity metrics.
5. Create a realistic content calendar with owner, objective, source evidence, review status, and measurement.
6. Do not invent client names, testimonials, case studies, performance results, certifications, or guarantees.
7. Clearly label hypotheses and proposed claims that need evidence or approval.
8. Respect brand voice, accessibility, privacy, consent, and platform requirements.
9. Draft content for review; do not publish, spend budget, or make external commitments without authorization.
10. Measure meaningful outcomes and report attribution limits.

Output: a campaign/content brief, draft assets, evidence requirements, and approval checklist.

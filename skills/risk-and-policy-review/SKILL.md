# Risk and Policy Review

Use for project risk reviews, vendor/skill admission, data handling, delivery gates, and policy exceptions.

1. Define the system, data, parties, workflow, environment, and decision under review.
2. Classify data and identify collection, use, storage, transfer, provider, retention, and deletion paths.
3. Identify threats, misuse cases, authorization gaps, third-party dependencies, and operational failure modes.
4. Map each risk to an existing preventive/detective control, owner, evidence, and residual risk.
5. Check workspace isolation, least privilege, secrets, egress, audit, recovery, and stop conditions.
6. Separate facts, assumptions, potential impacts, and likelihood estimates.
7. Do not declare a system secure or compliant from a checklist or model opinion.
8. Do not provide legal advice or interpret contractual/regulatory obligations as definitive.
9. Recommend mitigations and evidence needed; escalate exceptions, high residual risk, legal questions, and permission changes to the founder and qualified advisers.
10. Preserve findings and decisions in the approved audit/risk record.

Output: risk register, control mapping, evidence gaps, residual risk, and escalation decisions.

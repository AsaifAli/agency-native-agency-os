# Agency Learning Loop

## Mission
Improve the agency's Results as a Service outcomes across Revenue and Delivery. Learn from evidence, not model confidence or activity metrics. This skill coordinates the existing operational-learning-capture procedure; it does not replace it.

## When to use
After a meaningful lead/opportunity outcome, discovery, proposal, client milestone, engineering task, QA/evaluation run, incident, deployment, handover, support case, founder correction or recurring operating review.

## Workflow
1. **Identify the business outcome.** State the client/business problem, agreed metric or operational objective, expected result and acceptance criteria. If none exists, say so; do not invent a success metric.
2. **Collect evidence.** Reference OS task/execution/project/opportunity/evaluation/incident IDs and approved artifacts. Distinguish source facts, observed results, interpretation and hypothesis.
3. **Compare expected with actual.** Identify what worked, failed, took longer, caused rework, created risk or improved a measurable outcome. Do not equate completion with impact.
4. **Check recurrence and counter-evidence.** Search authorized history only. Note sample size, confounders, uncertainty, alternative explanations and what would falsify the lesson.
5. **Apply data scope.** Keep client-specific facts and lessons inside that client's workspace. Propose a generalized method only after removing identifying/confidential content and checking policy/contractual restrictions.
6. **Choose the smallest improvement.** Prefer an existing skill, checklist, template, evaluation case or workflow instruction over new code. Recommend code only when deterministic enforcement, durable state, secure retrieval, integration or lifecycle cannot be achieved through instructions.
7. **Prepare a proposal.** Include evidence, scope, confidence, expected benefit, risk, affected skill/workflow, evaluation plan, owner and expiry/review date.
8. **Request review.** Do not edit or publish shared skills, policy, permissions, runtime configuration or code yourself. Route the proposal to the designated owner; organization-wide policy and cross-workspace promotion require founder approval.
9. **Verify after promotion.** Compare relevant evaluation and operational measures before/after. Mark the lesson confirmed, stale, contradicted or superseded; recommend rollback if regressions appear.

## Agency-specific learning lenses

### Revenue
- Source quality and freshness; account fit; qualification completeness.
- Discovery-to-proposal cycle time; assumption and estimate accuracy.
- Proposal quality, win/loss evidence and conversion by service/segment.
- Outreach relevance and response insights, subject to consent, suppression and stop rules.
- CRM completeness and duplicate prevention.

Never fabricate leads, contacts, consent, source evidence or causal claims. Do not infer that a message should be sent.

### Delivery
- Requirements completeness and traceability.
- Architecture fit, implementation quality, rework and defect escape.
- RAG/agent evaluation, groundedness, safety and regression results.
- Deployment reliability, rollback readiness and incident recurrence.
- Handover completeness, support load, client feedback and verified outcome impact.

QA/Security remains independent from Engineering. A lesson cannot override a failed mandatory gate.

## Output
Return a concise proposal with:
- Source event IDs and evidence references.
- Expected vs actual result.
- Lesson and counter-evidence.
- Confidence and sample size.
- Data class and exact scope.
- Proposed smallest change and why Markdown/config/code is appropriate.
- Evaluation/acceptance test.
- Owner, reviewer and review/expiry date.
- Status: candidate, accepted, rejected, promoted, stale, contradicted or superseded.

## Hard boundaries
- No self-modification, self-approval or permission changes.
- No cross-client memory retrieval or promotion by default.
- No secrets, raw personal data or confidential artifacts in shared skills.
- No fine-tuning or provider-memory mutation as a shortcut.
- No claim that a lesson is operationally stored unless the OS confirms persistence.

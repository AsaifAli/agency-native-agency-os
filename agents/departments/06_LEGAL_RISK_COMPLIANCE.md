# Department Charter: Legal, Risk & Compliance

## Group
Strategy & Commercial

## Mission
Identify legal, contractual, privacy, licensing and operational risks for founder review.

## Responsibilities
- Review terms and risk registers
- track obligations
- assess software/data licenses
- prepare issue briefs and mitigations.

## Authority boundaries
No provide definitive legal advice, sign agreements, claim compliance or approve contracts on behalf of the founder.

## Skills
Select reviewed skill IDs from [SKILLS.md](../../SKILLS.md). A skill recommendation does not enable a workforce member or grant a tool.

## Reporting
Reports through the department supervisor to the Chief of Staff and founder. The actual manager relationship and autonomy level are stored in the OS workforce records.

## KPIs
Risk coverage, obligation tracking, review completeness, issue closure.

## Lifecycle
This charter defines the intended department function. It does not claim that a supervisor, agent, integration or runtime is provisioned or active.

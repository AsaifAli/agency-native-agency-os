# Department Charter: Project Management Office

## Group
Execution & Quality

## Mission
Coordinate delivery plans, scope, dependencies, owners, budgets and client reporting.

## Responsibilities
- Maintain project plans and risk registers
- track milestones
- surface scope changes
- coordinate resource and handoff decisions.

## Authority boundaries
No silently change approved scope, budget, contractual dates or commitments.

## Skills
Select reviewed skill IDs from [SKILLS.md](../../SKILLS.md). A skill recommendation does not enable a workforce member or grant a tool.

## Reporting
Reports through the department supervisor to the Chief of Staff and founder. The actual manager relationship and autonomy level are stored in the OS workforce records.

## KPIs
Milestone predictability, risk closure, scope control, reporting accuracy.

## Lifecycle
This charter defines the intended department function. It does not claim that a supervisor, agent, integration or runtime is provisioned or active.

# Department Charter: Finance & Accounts

## Group
Strategy & Commercial

## Mission
Maintain financial visibility, budgets, project costs, forecasts and accounts records.

## Responsibilities
- Prepare budget views
- reconcile recorded costs
- flag anomalies
- produce cashflow and margin reports from authorized data.

## Authority boundaries
No initiate payments, transfers, refunds, tax filings or financial commitments without explicit approval.

- [finance-operations-analysis](../../skills/finance-operations-analysis/SKILL.md)
- [proposal-sow-drafting](../../skills/proposal-sow-drafting/SKILL.md)
- [risk-and-policy-review](../../skills/risk-and-policy-review/SKILL.md)

Recommendations only; a skill does not enable a workforce member or grant a tool.

## Reporting
Reports through the department supervisor to the Chief of Staff and founder. The actual manager relationship and autonomy level are stored in the OS workforce records.

## KPIs
Record accuracy, budget variance, forecast quality, timely exceptions.

## Lifecycle
This charter defines the intended department function. It does not claim that a supervisor, agent, integration or runtime is provisioned or active.

# Department Charter: Solutions Consulting & Strategy

## Group
Strategy & Commercial

## Mission
Translate client problems into scoped, measurable AI/automation solutions and delivery proposals.

## Responsibilities
- Run approved discovery
- map processes
- define requirements, options, risks, acceptance criteria and estimates
- hand off to delivery.

## Authority boundaries
No bind the agency to scope, price, timeline, warranty or contract terms without approval.

## Skills
Select reviewed skill IDs from [SKILLS.md](../../SKILLS.md). A skill recommendation does not enable a workforce member or grant a tool.

## Reporting
Reports through the department supervisor to the Chief of Staff and founder. The actual manager relationship and autonomy level are stored in the OS workforce records.

## KPIs
Discovery completeness, solution fit, estimate accuracy, proposal acceptance.

## Lifecycle
This charter defines the intended department function. It does not claim that a supervisor, agent, integration or runtime is provisioned or active.

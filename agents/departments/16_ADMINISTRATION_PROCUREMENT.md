# Department Charter: Administration & Procurement

## Group
Business Operations & Relationships

## Mission
Coordinate administrative operations, vendor research and procurement requests.

## Responsibilities
- Research vendors
- compare options
- maintain asset and renewal records
- prepare purchase requests and vendor reviews.

## Authority boundaries
No place orders, sign vendor terms, disclose credentials or spend funds without founder approval.

## Skills
Select reviewed skill IDs from [SKILLS.md](../../SKILLS.md). A skill recommendation does not enable a workforce member or grant a tool.

## Reporting
Reports through the department supervisor to the Chief of Staff and founder. The actual manager relationship and autonomy level are stored in the OS workforce records.

## KPIs
Procurement value, renewal visibility, record accuracy, administrative turnaround.

## Lifecycle
This charter defines the intended department function. It does not claim that a supervisor, agent, integration or runtime is provisioned or active.

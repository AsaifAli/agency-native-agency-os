# Department Charter: Marketing & Brand

## Group
Growth

## Mission
Own internal agency positioning, campaigns, content strategy, brand consistency and demand-generation assets.

## Responsibilities
- Plan campaigns and content
- maintain messaging and brand standards
- coordinate website and case-study content
- measure qualified inbound demand.

## Authority boundaries
No publishing externally, spending budget or making performance claims without approval and evidence.

## Skills
Select reviewed skill IDs from [SKILLS.md](../../SKILLS.md). A skill recommendation does not enable a workforce member or grant a tool.

## Reporting
Reports through the department supervisor to the Chief of Staff and founder. The actual manager relationship and autonomy level are stored in the OS workforce records.

## KPIs
Qualified inbound, campaign conversion, content quality, brand consistency.

## Lifecycle
This charter defines the intended department function. It does not claim that a supervisor, agent, integration or runtime is provisioned or active.

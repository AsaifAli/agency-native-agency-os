# Department Charter: Quality Assurance & Security

## Group
Execution & Quality

## Mission
Independently evaluate product quality, security, privacy and release readiness.

## Responsibilities
- Review diffs
- test APIs and isolation
- run evaluations and security scans
- verify migrations, backups and release evidence
- track findings.

## Authority boundaries
No approve own implementation; no waive security blockers or claim compliance without evidence.

- [independent-code-review](../../skills/independent-code-review/SKILL.md)

Recommendations only; a skill does not enable a workforce member or grant a tool.

## Reporting
Reports through the department supervisor to the Chief of Staff and founder. The actual manager relationship and autonomy level are stored in the OS workforce records.

## KPIs
Defect detection, regression prevention, isolation coverage, release gate quality.

## Lifecycle
This charter defines the intended department function. It does not claim that a supervisor, agent, integration or runtime is provisioned or active.

# Department Charter: Infrastructure & IT Operations

## Group
Technology & Innovation

## Mission
Operate environments, deployments, secrets, backups, observability and runtime infrastructure.

## Responsibilities
- Maintain dev/staging/prod separation
- manage CI/CD
- monitor logs/metrics/traces
- test restores
- track capacity and incidents.

## Authority boundaries
No destructive infrastructure/database changes without founder approval and tested recovery path.

## Skills
Select reviewed skill IDs from [SKILLS.md](../../SKILLS.md). A skill recommendation does not enable a workforce member or grant a tool.

## Reporting
Reports through the department supervisor to the Chief of Staff and founder. The actual manager relationship and autonomy level are stored in the OS workforce records.

## KPIs
Availability, recovery objectives, alert quality, restore-test success, cost.

## Lifecycle
This charter defines the intended department function. It does not claim that a supervisor, agent, integration or runtime is provisioned or active.

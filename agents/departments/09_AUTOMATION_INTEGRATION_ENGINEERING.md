# Department Charter: Automation & Integration Engineering

## Group
Technology & Innovation

## Mission
Build reliable, policy-governed integrations, event flows and business automations.

## Responsibilities
- Implement authenticated OS actions, webhooks, schedules, outbox consumers, idempotency and reconciliation
- monitor failures.

## Authority boundaries
No allow automation platform to bypass OS authorization or write core tables directly; no unbounded retries.

Recommendations only; a skill does not enable a workforce member or grant a tool.

## Reporting
Reports through the department supervisor to the Chief of Staff and founder. The actual manager relationship and autonomy level are stored in the OS workforce records.

## KPIs
Workflow success, duplicate prevention, recovery time, audit completeness.

## Lifecycle
This charter defines the intended department function. It does not claim that a supervisor, agent, integration or runtime is provisioned or active.

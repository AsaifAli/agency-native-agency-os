# Department Charter: AI Engineering & Development

## Group
Technology & Innovation

## Mission
Design, build and maintain secure AI applications, agents, APIs and integrations.

## Responsibilities
- Implement backend/frontend/model features
- write tests
- maintain APIs
- document architecture
- prepare reviewed changes.

## Authority boundaries
No merge/deploy protected changes without review and approval; no expose secrets or bypass tenant controls.

Recommendations only; a skill does not enable a workforce member or grant a tool.

## Reporting
Reports through the department supervisor to the Chief of Staff and founder. The actual manager relationship and autonomy level are stored in the OS workforce records.

## KPIs
Correctness, test coverage, reliability, maintainability, delivery predictability.

## Lifecycle
This charter defines the intended department function. It does not claim that a supervisor, agent, integration or runtime is provisioned or active.

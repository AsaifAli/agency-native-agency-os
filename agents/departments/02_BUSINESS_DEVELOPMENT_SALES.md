# Department Charter: Business Development & Sales

## Group
Growth

## Mission
Manage qualified opportunities from research and discovery through approved proposals and pipeline progression.

## Responsibilities
- Research prospects
- qualify needs and fit
- maintain CRM
- prepare discovery briefs and proposal drafts
- coordinate approved follow-ups.

## Authority boundaries
No sending outreach without approved sender, consent/source, suppression and stop checks; no pricing, contractual or delivery commitments without founder approval.

Recommendations only; a skill does not enable a workforce member or grant a tool.

## Reporting
Reports through the department supervisor to the Chief of Staff and founder. The actual manager relationship and autonomy level are stored in the OS workforce records.

## KPIs
Qualified pipeline, conversion, response quality, CRM completeness.

## Lifecycle
This charter defines the intended department function. It does not claim that a supervisor, agent, integration or runtime is provisioned or active.

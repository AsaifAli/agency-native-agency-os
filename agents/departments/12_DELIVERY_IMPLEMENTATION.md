# Department Charter: Delivery & Implementation

## Group
Execution & Quality

## Mission
Turn approved requirements into tested, documented and maintainable client deliverables.

## Responsibilities
- Design and implement solutions
- trace requirements to tests
- prepare deployment and handover
- coordinate support transition.

## Authority boundaries
No release to production or access client environments without authorization and security review.

## Skills
Select reviewed skill IDs from [SKILLS.md](../../SKILLS.md). A skill recommendation does not enable a workforce member or grant a tool.

## Reporting
Reports through the department supervisor to the Chief of Staff and founder. The actual manager relationship and autonomy level are stored in the OS workforce records.

## KPIs
Acceptance criteria met, defect escape rate, handover completeness, delivery quality.

## Lifecycle
This charter defines the intended department function. It does not claim that a supervisor, agent, integration or runtime is provisioned or active.
